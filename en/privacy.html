2) Contact

When you get in touch with us (for example, via a form or email), we collect the personal data you provide. This data is used exclusively to respond to your request and for related administrative management.

The legal basis applicable is art. 7, item IX (legitimate interest) of the LGPD, and, when the contact has a contractual purpose, art. 7, item V (performance of a contract) also applies.

The data will be deleted after the request is closed, unless a legal or regulatory obligation requires its retention.

3) Data Processing for Contract Performance

3.1.

To enable the app to work and to perform the contracted services, we may share data with essential providers, such as:

  • companies responsible for processing payments;
  • intermediary platforms used to manage subscriptions;
  • technical partners required to deliver the app's features.

Sharing occurs on the basis of art. 7, item V (performance of a contract) of the LGPD.

3.2. RevenueCat (in-app purchases)

When you make in-app purchases or subscriptions, processing is handled by RevenueCat Inc., based in San Francisco, USA. We transmit to RevenueCat the data essential for payment processing and subscription validation.

This data is processed on the basis of art. 7, item V (performance of a contract) of the LGPD.

We maintain a contract with RevenueCat providing for the protection of information and prohibition of improper use.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4) Data Subject Rights

Under the LGPD, you have the right to:

  • Confirmation of the existence of processing (art. 18, I)
  • Access to your data (art. 18, II)
  • Correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data (art. 18, III)
  • Anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data (art. 18, IV)
  • Portability, when regulated by the ANPD (art. 18, V)
  • Deletion of personal data processed with consent (art. 18, VI)
  • Information about data sharing (art. 18, VII)
  • Information about the possibility of not providing consent and its consequences (art. 18, VIII)
  • Revocation of consent (art. 18, §5º)
  • Opposition to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interest (art. 18, §2º)

Requests can be made directly to the controller by email.

5) International Data Transfer

Some services used by the app (such as Firebase and RevenueCat) are located in the United States or in other countries.

International transfer occurs under the terms of art. 33, item IX of the LGPD (cooperation with essential providers) and through adequate contractual safeguards for protection.

6) Data Security

We adopt technical and administrative measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access and accidental or unlawful situations of destruction, loss, alteration, communication or disclosure.

7) Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time, always with the date of the changes highlighted at the end of the document.
